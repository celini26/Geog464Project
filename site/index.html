About this map

Made for young parents and families to find schools with nearby age-appropriate recreation.

How to use it

The map starts empty. Open the filter (☰) and tick school and recreation types to show them. Only schools with a matching recreation site within walking distance stay on the map. Click a school to see its closest sites and the walking route. Use the search (🔍) to find a school or recreation site by name.

Good to know

Distances are walking distances along streets and paths. Playgrounds for ages 18 months to 5 years only match preschool schools, and playgrounds for ages 5 to 12 only match primary schools. Seasons are estimated from the activity type; actual opening dates vary by borough.
