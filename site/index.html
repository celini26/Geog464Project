About this map

Made for young parents and families to find schools with nearby age-appropriate recreation.

How to use it

The map starts empty. Open the filter (☰) and tick school and recreation types to show them. Click a school to see its closest recreation sites within the distance you set. Use the search (🔍) to find a school or recreation site by name.
